For parents

20 to 30 minutes, together

A parent guide in every issue

How to guide your child step by step without taking over:

  • Let them predict first.
  • Hands off. Help only with the scissors.
  • Ask "What one thing could you change?"
  • Praise the effort, not the answer.

Plus 3 questions to ask at the dinner table.

Ages 4 to 12, with a separate edition for 4–6, 7–9 and 10–12.

No. Every page works on paper. The parent app is optional.

What materials are needed?

Only common household items: paper, tape, straws and pencils.

How long does an issue take?

About 20 to 30 minutes, or spread it across the weekend.

When does it arrive?

Every Saturday morning.

Can I pause or cancel?

Yes, anytime.

Is my child's data safe?

We only collect what we need. We ask for a parent's consent before printing any child's name or work.
